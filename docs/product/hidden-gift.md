# Hidden gift — POSTPONED

Decided 2026-11-14 to leave this for later. Don't build it yet.

## The idea (from the brainstorm)

- A gift (discount code etc.) hidden somewhere on the site.
- The first time, show: "You have 30 minutes to find a great gift from us — search the site."
  The brainstorm shows this when the visitor reaches the end of a category flow.
- After 30 minutes the gift downgrades to a simpler one.
- When found: a stylish card, heavily blurred, with a phone field under it. Enter the
  number → OTP → the card un-blurs and reveals the gift. This is also the login.

## Suggestions made (not yet agreed)

- Start the timer at the end of the first category's steps, as a small **non-blocking**
  card next to the CTA (not a modal that pulls people away from it). Keep a small countdown
  pill in the navbar afterwards.
- Avoid one fixed hiding spot (it gets shared online): ~5 possible spots, one picked per
  device; codes unique per phone number and single-use.
- Spot ideas that fit the brand: one star that blinks differently, a frame in the time
  tunnel, the logo's eye blinking after a long hover, a word on About.
- On mobile, give a hint after a few minutes.
- Timer stored per device; the real limit is one gift per phone number.
- Make the gift concrete: "X% off any fixed-price service", a free creative consultation,
  or a half-price first Brand Audit — not a vague discount on custom work.
