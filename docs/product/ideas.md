# Ideas for later

Ideas for distinctive experiences across the site, parked on 2026-11-15 so they aren't lost.
None of them is decided. Pick one up when its page is designed, and log the decision in
[../decisions.md](../decisions.md).

- **Contact as a screenplay page.** The request form is written like a script page
  (`INT. FAYA STUDIO — DAY`), and the visitor fills in blanks inside the sentences ("A *brand*
  needs a *film* by *March*"). It could become the template for every service's request form
  (see [requests-and-pricing.md](requests-and-pricing.md)).
- **About as end credits.** The team rolls past like a film's closing credits.
- **Gallery as a light table.** Works are laid out like a sheet of film negatives, a magnifier
  follows the cursor, and clicking a frame plays the work.
- **Pricing as a rate card.** Laid out like a production budget sheet, in mono type with line
  items.
- **Page changes as film burn.** A short burn-out or light leak between pages instead of a fade.
- **404 page: "Scene missing",** with a burned-out frame.
- **The hub sky follows Tehran's time of day.** Darker at night, with more meteors.
